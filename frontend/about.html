When the model declines

A loan is declined when the expected loss from default is larger than the fee it would earn. Longer loans earn more fees, so they can carry more risk.

Decline threshold by loan term
Loan termDeclines at or above
1 month7.5%
3 months19.6%
6 months32.8%

Assumes a fee of 7.5% a month, a funding cost of 1% a month, and that 80% of a defaulted loan is lost.

Risk bands

Bands describe the size of the risk. They are separate from the decision: a 6-month loan in the high band can still be approved.

Risk bands by chance of default
BandChance of default
LowUnder 10%
Medium10% to under 20%
High20% to under 35%
Very high35% and over

Known limits

  • Gender, country and age group are never inputs, but an audit found the model declines women and applicants aged 50 and over more often than other applicants with the same true risk.
  • For three inputs the model moves the score the wrong way. For example, more overdraft borrowing lowers it slightly.
  • The full model card in the project repository (docs/model_card.md) has the details.
